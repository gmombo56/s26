Meet the Team

Grace Mombo - Lead Designer

Grace Mombo is the lead designer and founder of theater 101. She has been involved with theater since the 4th grade. She initially planned to work as a software engineer but after getting more involved in club and community theater she became passionate about sharing the craft with other people. She hopes to show through this website that anyone can participate in theater at any level.

John Smith - Front End Developer

John Smith does the front-end development for theater 101. He did not have a lot of theater knowledge before starting this website but has learned a lot more through working with Grace. He worked as a software engineer for Microsoft before wanting to switch to a smaller startup so he brings a lot of connections to the technical world to the team.

Jane Doe - Back End Developer

Jane Doe is the back-end developer for theater 101. She met Grace through a freshman year coding class and they have been close friends since. Jane has helped Grace think through the early stages of the website. She was involved in theater in high school and continued in clubs throughout college helping Grace to form the content of the website.
